About Us

Our Story

Sallysam Limited is a trusted construction and contracting company based in Mombasa, Kenya. With expertise in general building, civil works, water works, and regravelling, we deliver high-quality projects that stand the test of time.

Our dedication to professionalism, safety, and innovation makes us the partner of choice for clients across the region. We specialize in delivering construction solutions that enhance infrastructure, support communities, and promote sustainable growth.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional construction solutions that enhance infrastructure, support communities, and promote sustainable growth across Kenya.

Our Vision

To be the leading construction and contracting company in East Africa, recognized for our integrity, innovation, and commitment to quality.

Our Services

1. General Building

We design, construct, and renovate residential, commercial, and industrial buildings. Our team ensures every structure is built to the highest standards of quality and durability, tailored to meet the specific needs of our clients.

2. Civil Works

From road construction to drainage systems, we handle large-scale civil engineering projects with precision. We are equipped to deliver infrastructure that supports community growth and economic development.

3. Water Works

We provide reliable water supply systems, borehole drilling, and drainage solutions for residential and commercial properties. Ensuring access to clean and sustainable water resources is a core part of our infrastructure commitment.

4. Regravelling

We offer professional regravelling services to restore and maintain gravel roads and pathways. This service improves accessibility, prevents erosion, and extends the lifespan of rural and estate road networks.

The Role of Civil Works in Building Better Communities

Introduction: Civil works go beyond roads and bridges—they form the backbone of thriving, functional communities. From the drainage systems that keep our streets safe during heavy rains to the road networks that connect businesses and families, robust infrastructure is essential. At Sallysam Limited, we take pride in delivering civil engineering projects that connect people and boost economic growth.

Key Points:

  • Road Networks: Well-constructed roads enhance trade, reduce vehicle wear and tear, and improve accessibility for both residential and commercial areas.
  • Drainage Systems: Proper drainage prevents flooding, protects property foundations, and improves overall community sanitation and health.
  • Community Impact: Civil works improve mobility, safety, and social development, creating an environment where businesses and families can thrive.
  • Our Expertise: From drainage to road regravelling, Sallysam ensures high-quality, long-lasting results using modern engineering techniques and durable materials.

Conclusion: Civil works shape the way communities grow and prosper. By choosing Sallysam Limited, you’re choosing infrastructure that supports progress and enhances the quality of life for generations to come.

Building Sustainable Homes – The Future of Construction in Kenya

Introduction: The future of construction is green, and sustainable homes are at the heart of this global shift. As climate patterns change and resources become scarcer, the demand for eco-friendly building practices has never been higher. At Sallysam Limited, we are embracing sustainable construction methods to ensure durability, comfort, and environmental responsibility in all our projects.

Key Points:

  • Energy Efficiency: Modern homes require less energy through smart design, proper insulation, and natural ventilation, significantly reducing electricity costs.
  • Water Management: Our water works projects promote conservation and reliable access, incorporating rainwater harvesting and efficient drainage solutions.
  • Sustainable Materials: Using durable, locally sourced, low-impact materials reduces waste, lowers carbon footprints, and minimizes transportation costs.
  • Why It Matters: Sustainable homes save money on utilities, protect the environment for future generations, and increase the long-term value of the property.

Conclusion: Kenya’s construction industry is rapidly evolving, and Sallysam Limited is leading the way with sustainable solutions that benefit homeowners, communities, and the environment alike.
